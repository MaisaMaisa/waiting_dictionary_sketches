I am waiting for the house prices to go down so that I am able to buy a house. I’ve been waiting on this for a long time.

I am waiting for my visa papers since November last year. I was approved for a visa but I am still waiting to receive the documents. I cannot travel while I don’t have this document. I called the IND and they were very rude, telling me that in the Netherlands we don’t interrupt people. I filed a complaint.

I am waiting for my allowance to arrive in my bank account. I have been waiting for two months instead of one, because I filled the paper a bit too late for being paid accordingly. I experience it as a stressful experience because I need to pay back my friend whom I lended money to. I feel alone in this process.

I am waiting for a package that was sent by my mother two weeks ago. It contains memories from my home room so that we feel together even apart. I am excited to receive it but also worried as it is not arriving and as I did not pay for the tracking package. I feel connected to my mother while waiting, we call and text meanwhile. I spend more time at home waiting for it to arrive.
